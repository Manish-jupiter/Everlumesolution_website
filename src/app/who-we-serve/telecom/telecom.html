Case Study

Agentic AI for Telecom Operations & Customer Care

  1. Step 01

    Business Challenge

    A leading telecommunications provider serving millions of subscribers was experiencing growing operational pressures driven by increasing customer interactions, expanding network complexity, and rising service expectations. Customer support teams were struggling with escalating call volumes, delayed service activations, billing enquiries, and prolonged issue resolution times, while the NOC was overwhelmed by repetitive alerts, manual triage, and fragmented processes.

    Critical workflows were spread across multiple OSS/BSS platforms, CRM applications, billing systems, service management, and network monitoring solutions — resulting in inconsistent incident handling, delayed handoffs, limited visibility, and heavy manual intervention. As service demand continued to grow, costs climbed, satisfaction declined, and meeting SLAs became increasingly difficult.

  2. Step 02

    Solution Delivered

    We implemented an enterprise-grade Agentic AI platform that unified customer care, service operations, and network management through intelligent workflow orchestration and AI-assisted decision-making — integrated with OSS/BSS, CRM, billing, order management, network monitoring, service assurance, and digital customer engagement channels as a single execution layer.

    Specialised AI agents continuously monitored customer interactions, network events, service requests, and operational workflows — classifying incidents, correlating events, identifying root causes, and orchestrating the best resolution path. Routine enquiries (activations, plan changes, billing, account requests, common technical issues) were resolved autonomously within governance policies, while complex or customer-sensitive cases were escalated.

    The platform also leveraged AI-powered knowledge retrieval, intelligent case prioritisation, predictive incident management, and explainable decision support — accelerating resolution while preserving governance, service quality, and customer trust.

  3. Step 03

    Key Capabilities

    • Unified customer care, service assurance & network ops via intelligent orchestration.
    • AI-powered classification, prioritisation & routing of requests and incidents.
    • Automated service provisioning, activation, billing & routine service workflows.
    • Event correlation & root-cause analysis across monitoring and assurance platforms.
    • Predictive identification of disruptions & proactive customer communication.
    • Human-in-the-loop escalation for high-priority & complex scenarios.
    • Real-time dashboards for service health, SLAs, incidents & workflow bottlenecks.
    • Comprehensive audit trails, governance & explainable AI recommendations.
  4. Step 04

    Business Outcomes

    Significantly reduced detection, triage & resolution times across care and network ops.

    Improved first-contact resolution rates & overall customer satisfaction.

    Reduced workload for support, NOC & field operations teams through automation.

    Increased service reliability & resilience through AI-driven orchestration.

    Real-time insights into network performance, service quality & operations.

    Lower operating cost while enabling scalable, high-quality service delivery.
